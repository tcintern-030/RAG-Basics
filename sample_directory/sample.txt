Artificial Intelligence (AI) is changing the way people work and live.
AI is used in healthcare, education, finance, transportation, and many other fields.

Machine Learning is a branch of AI that enables computers to learn from data.
Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks.

Large Language Models (LLMs) such as Gemini, GPT, and Claude can understand and generate human language.
These models are useful for chatbots, summarization, translation, and question answering.

Text splitting is an important step in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
It divides long documents into smaller chunks before generating embeddings.
